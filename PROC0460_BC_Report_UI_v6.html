B&C Detection Methodology — v6
PROC0460 · FortifAI Veritas
Counsel Reference. Two-arm detection architecture, keyword taxonomy, trusted vendor capping, three-stage workflow, and audit trail. Prepared under attorney–client privilege.
1
Two-Arm Detection Architecture
Veritas runs two parallel detection arms against every P2P transaction. Either arm can fire independently; transactions firing both arms ("Combined") receive the highest priority.
ArmDetection Basis
Arm 1 — TextInvoice text matched against 8-category keyword taxonomy
Arm 2 — AttributeVendor attribute risk (individual name, new, SOD overlap, etc.) — no keyword required
CombinedBoth arms fired — highest investigative priority

5
Three-Stage Investigation Workflow
Every flagged anomaly moves through a deterministic workflow with three states:
StageActorActionsNext State
Risk WorkbenchInitial Reviewer (CAE / Internal Audit)Investigate, Block Payment, Share, Dismiss, Escalate→ Dismissed OR → Privileged Review
Privileged ReviewGeneral CounselConfirm Risk, Dispose, Return to Workbench→ Dismissed (GC Reviewed) OR back to Workbench
DismissedTerminalRestore (override)Permanent audit record
All transitions recorded in anomaly_item_disposition. Privileged Review dispositions carry the GC Reviewed label and are protected by attorney–client privilege.
6
Dismissal Reasons & Engine Feedback
Dismissal reasons fall into two groups. False Positive reasons feed the monthly engine retraining loop. GC dispositions are legal determinations and do not retrain detection.

7
Share & Audit Trail
Reviewers can share any transaction with internal or external recipients (e.g. external counsel, Big 4 forensic partners) via copy link, email, or message. Every share requires explicit confirmation that the recipient is authorised to receive privileged information. All shares are logged with full metadata payload.
Production: share links carry a token with expiry, recipient lock, and watermarked PDF/HTML rendering. Audit log written to privileged_share_log.
8
Live A&B Integration
Veritas reads TRAE via LEFT JOIN to anomaly_item_disposition. Dismissed and escalated items auto-removed from active workbench. Disposition state syncs both ways with the A&B Drill-Down module.
TRAE is append-only. Dispositions stored separately. Exclusion filter applied at query time.
