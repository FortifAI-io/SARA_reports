Methodology
Pattern baselines 0
Reviewers & routing 0
Three detection methods. Rule-based controls test fixed conditions. Pattern analysis learns each account's normal from history and flags deviation \u2014 applied at entry level and at balance level. Reconciliation and coherence compare a balance to something outside its own history, and are the forward direction.

How the agent runs

Read-only, no write access. Fortifai's connectors read the ledger and write nothing back to SAP. No change to how entries are posted; no dependency on the SAP change process.
Continuous refresh, set by Honeywell. The connectors read newly posted and changed data daily or weekly \u2014 which is what shifts the agent from after-the-fact detection to enabling correction while the period is still open.
Detect, alert and route \u2014 not block. Every item is scored, assigned to a reviewer by role, and where risky, alerted immediately to the person who can act. The agent informs the close; it never holds a posting.
In-scope account classes. Accruals & provisions, control accounts, prepaids & deferrals, intercompany, and suspense & clearing. Cash & bank, fixed assets and equity & reserves are out of scope for the current phases, as they depend on reconciliation and roll-forward methods.
Posting-period discipline (US GAAP). Every entry carries its fiscal year and posting period (BKPF.gjahr / monat). C11 flags entries posted into a closed or special year-end adjustment period (P13–P16) — ASC 250 prior-period-adjustment territory. Fortifai flags and evidences; the ASC 250 determination rests with management and its auditors.
Boundary statement. This surface presents signals and evidence for review \u2014 not a control conclusion. Pattern analysis establishes that an item is unusual against history; it does not establish that it is wrong. For Honeywell as a listed entity, determinations of control effectiveness rest with management and its external auditors.
Pattern baselines. What the engine considers normal for each account, learned from its own history. This is the reference the pattern methods score against \u2014 shown so that a reviewer, and an auditor, can see why an item was judged unusual.
