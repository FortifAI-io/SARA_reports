B&C Detection & Due-Diligence Methodology — v7
PROC0460 · FortifAI Veritas + D7 · Prepared under attorney–client privilege
Counsel Reference. This document describes the complete two-stage methodology: Stage 1 transaction-level bribery & corruption screening (Veritas) and Stage 2 counterparty due diligence (D7). It is written to support regulatory and legal scrutiny under the FCPA, UK Bribery Act 2010, and equivalent anti-corruption regimes. All automated outputs are risk signals requiring human verification, never determinations of wrongdoing. Losses are described as potential. Every disposition is made by a qualified reviewer and recorded for audit.
1
Regulatory Basis & Scope
PROC0460 screens 100% of vendor invoice postings for language and vendor characteristics consistent with bribery, corruption, or improper facilitation payments. It operates as continuous monitoring over the SAP Procure-to-Pay population and does not determine that bribery has occurred; it identifies transactions and counterparties warranting further investigation.
FrameworkProvisionHow addressed
FCPA §13(b)(2)(A)Books & records — accurate reflection of transactionsText taxonomy detects mischaracterised, vague, or disguised expenditure (Categories B/D/F/H)
FCPA §13(b)(2)(B)Internal accounting controlsControl-failure detection: payment-block bypass, SoD overlap, vendor-master change evidence
UK Bribery Act 2010 §7Failure to prevent bribery — "adequate procedures" defenceDemonstrable continuous monitoring over payment populations exposed to corruption risk
COSO 2013 · Principle 8Assesses fraud riskTwo-arm detection plus counterparty due diligence across the vendor lifecycle
IIA GIAS 2024 · Std 9.4 / SOX 404Assurance over ICFRAuditable evidence trail; disposition register; privileged review workflow
2
Two-Stage Architecture
Risk is assessed at two distinct levels. Stage 1 is transaction-level (does this payment exhibit B&C indicators?). Stage 2 is counterparty-level (is this vendor an integrity risk?). The two stages are complementary: a transaction can be clean against a high-risk counterparty, or suspicious against a well-verified one. Combining both is what differentiates this methodology from transaction-monitoring-only or KYC-only approaches.
StageUnitEngineQuestion answered
Stage 1FI accounting document lineVeritas (PROC0460)Does this transaction show bribery/corruption indicators?
Stage 2Vendor (counterparty)D7 Due DiligenceIs this counterparty an integrity, sanctions, PEP, or related-party risk?
Every unique vendor appearing in a Stage 1 flag is automatically queued for Stage 2 D7 screening. Stage 2 findings are surfaced back onto Stage 1 transactions via the D7 badge in the Risk Workbench, so an investigator sees counterparty integrity without leaving the transaction.
3
Stage 1 — Two-Arm Detection
Veritas runs two parallel detection arms against every P2P transaction. A record is flagged when either arm fires. When both fire on the same record the detection arm is "Combined" and the risk tier is Critical.
ArmBasisFires when
Arm 1 — TextInvoice text matched against the 8-category B&C keyword taxonomy on up to four text fields, with negative-context exclusions and compound patternsA keyword matches and the transaction amount meets the category threshold
Arm 2 — AttributeTen vendor attribute indicators across a Strong / Weak tier model≥3 total indicators AND at least one Strong indicator (see §5)
CombinedBoth arms fire on the same lineAutomatic Critical — highest investigative priority
4
Keyword Taxonomy (8 Categories)
Categories are evaluated in priority order; the highest-priority match is the primary category, and all matches are retained for review. Each category carries an amount threshold and a severity ceiling. Negative-context terms suppress false matches (e.g. "gift to employee"); compound patterns require co-occurrence (e.g. "travel" + "officer") to capture context-specific corruption risk.
CatNameThresholdCeilingIllustrative patterns
HGovernment & Regulatory Channel1 (no de minimis)Criticalgovt fee, tender, licence, permit, customs, CSD/CPC, facilitation
ADirect Bribery Indicators5,000Criticalgift, donation, commission, gold/silver bar, hamper, sponsorship
DEvents & Festivals10,000Medium (D11→Critical)diwali, pooja, festival, mela; +govt vocabulary = D11
GProperty, Land & Regulatory25,000Highstamp duty, property rent, court fee, NOC
FVague Descriptors10,000Mediummiscellaneous, reimbursement, imprest, on behalf of
EMarketing & Incentives50,000Mediumpromotion, incentive, bonus, reward, loyalty
BProfessional Services50,000Highconsultancy, retainer, professional fee, placement, notary
CTravel & Hospitality100,000Mediumhotel, air ticket, accommodation, lodging
Category H threshold is deliberately 1 (no de minimis). Facilitation payments to officials are small by design and are the highest FCPA enforcement risk. False positives from routine statutory payments are controlled through negative-context exclusions and the trusted-vendor allowlist, not by raising the amount floor. Cross-category compounds H10 (govt + vague), H11 (govt + commission) and D11 (festival + government) escalate to Critical.
5
Arm 2 — Tiered Vendor Indicators
Ten indicators are split into two tiers by signal quality. Arm 2 fires only when at least one Strong indicator is present and the total count is ≥3. Weak-only combinations never fire Arm 2 independently, because structural vendor demographics (individual name, missing tax registration) are common in normal populations and are not, by themselves, evidence of corruption. This tiering is the primary control against false-positive inflation.
TierIndicators
Strong (≥1 required)High-risk country · one-time vendor · KYC change after PO · bank change ≤30d · sole-source at plant · amount > high-risk threshold · no-PO direct FI entry
Weak (cannot fire alone)New vendor (<90d) · individual-name vendor · no KYC (both PAN and tax-ID missing)
"No KYC" requires both primary tax identifiers to be absent (logical AND), so a vendor legitimately below a registration threshold is not flagged on that basis alone. The individual-name test uses an expanded corporate-suffix dictionary covering international and regional business-naming conventions.
6
Risk Tiering, Compounding & Confidence
Each flagged record receives a risk tier (Critical / High / Medium / Low) and a numeric score. Compounding patterns — combinations such as paid + control-bypassed, or paid + SoD overlap — escalate risk. Critically, compounding escalates to Critical only where text evidence exists (Combined or Arm 1). For Arm 2-only records, compounding escalates to High maximum: a counterparty flagged purely on attributes cannot reach Critical without corroborating transaction text. Operator-concentration does not escalate Arm 2-only records at all.
TierRepresentative basis
CriticalCombined arm · Category H · Category A + high amount · SoD critical overlap · chain-control breach · compounds (C8/D11/A14/H10/H11) · (paid + bypass/SoD/KYC-change, text-backed)
HighCategory A/B/G · SoD high overlap · Arm 2-only with ≥4 indicators or ≥2 Strong · Arm 2-only escalated by compounding
MediumCategories C/D/E/F · Arm 2-only default
LowResidual · trusted-vendor capped
Detection Confidence (0–100). Independent of impact severity, a confidence score measures how strong the evidence is that a record is genuinely B&C-related — combining detection-arm quality, keyword specificity, indicator depth, multi-field corroboration and rare-indicator presence. Investigators sort by confidence within a tier so the strongest signals are reviewed first. Payment status amplifies the impact score (+10 when paid, reflecting the shift from "at risk" to potential Cash Out).
7
Trusted-Vendor Capping & Suppression Governance
Established counterparties with structurally lower corruption risk (major audit firms, listed consultancies, media agencies, Tier-1 law firms, logistics, quick-commerce) are capped at Low/25 and excluded from the active workbench. Capping is annotation, not deletion — capped records remain fully visible in source data and the cap is defeated by any override condition.
Cap override (any one defeats the cap)
Segregation-of-duties overlap involving the vendor creator
Vendor KYC field change after the first PO commitment
Vendor bank-account change within 30 days of payment
A dismissal is a one-off false-positive judgement; an exclusion is a permanent pattern suppression. Both are recorded with reason, reviewer and timestamp. Only False-Positive dismissal reasons feed the monthly engine-tuning loop; General Counsel dispositions are legal determinations and never retrain detection.

D7 Global Screening Framework
D7 performs entity-level due diligence on every counterparty Veritas flags. The framework is jurisdiction-abstracted: each vendor's country routes it to the appropriate corporate registry and tax-validation source, while sanctions, PEP and adverse-media screening apply globally. D7 discloses which sources were checked and, where a source is not applicable in a jurisdiction, records that transparently — screening is scoped correctly rather than silently skipped.
DimensionSources (representative)Scope
Corporate registry & UBOMCA/ROC (IN) · Companies House (UK) · State SoS + SEC EDGAR (US) · ACRA (SG) · DED/Free-Zone (AE) · Companies Registry (HK) · aggregator fallbackPer jurisdiction
Tax-identity validationPAN/GST (IN) · VAT/UTR (UK) · EIN/TIN (US) · UEN/GST (SG) · TRN (AE)Per jurisdiction
Sanctions & watchlistsOFAC SDN · EU Consolidated · UN Security Council · UK HMT/OFSIGlobal — a sanctioned party is sanctioned everywhere
PEP screeningDomestic / foreign / international-organisation PEP databases (FATF definitions)Global; jurisdiction sets domestic vs foreign
Adverse mediaCorruption, fraud, money-laundering, sanctions-evasion categories; multi-languageGlobal
Each counterparty receives a composite D7 grade: Clean (all dimensions pass), Caution (elevated indicator requiring review), Adverse (material finding — recommend escalation), or Unverified (verification incomplete on available data).
10
Related-Party Network Methodology
Related-party detection is a multi-source triangulation resolved into an entity graph. No single external source is authoritative; edges are drawn from six layers and weighted by confidence, then connected components with two or more vendors form a cluster. Related-party inference is probabilistic, not conclusive — a cluster is a prompt for investigation, not a finding of collusion.
#LayerSignal (confidence)
1Shared identity attributes (SAP-internal)Common bank account (BANKN), tax ID, address, phone, email — highest confidence
2Vendor–employee linkage (SAP cross-reference)Vendor bank/address matches an employee (HR) record — conflict-of-interest / kickback signal
3Beneficial ownership (external registry)Shared director, UBO >25% (FATF/EU), or common registered agent
4Name similarity & structureFuzzy legal-name matching — corroborating only
5Transactional behaviourSame creator, sequential onboarding, round-robin or mutually-exclusive invoicing — inference
6Graph resolutionWeighted edges → connected components; cluster scored by its strongest edge type
A cluster's combined flagged exposure is reported at the cluster level, because a ring of individually-immaterial vendors can be collectively material. In this dataset four clusters are demonstrated, the strongest being a shared-bank-account bullion ring and a vendor–employee bank match.
11
AI Risk Synthesis — Governance
D7 provides an AI-generated risk synthesis that correlates Stage 1 transaction signals with Stage 2 counterparty findings into a plain-language assessment for the investigator and General Counsel. The AI is used for synthesis and prioritisation, not adjudication.
The AI doesThe AI does not
Summarise the combined Stage 1 + Stage 2 evidence in reviewable languageAssert that bribery or fraud has occurred
Describe indicators and recommend a proportionate next stepMake or record dispositions — every disposition is a human decision
Operate on the vendor's own evidence within the deploymentInvent facts beyond the provided evidence; signals-not-verdicts language is enforced
The synthesis runs on the deployment's model under zero-data-egress constraints. Its output is labelled as an AI assessment, is fully reviewable, and does not alter risk scores. Human-in-the-loop is mandatory for all escalation and disposition decisions.
12
Limitations & Evidentiary Posture
To support legal and regulatory scrutiny, the methodology is explicit about its limits:
LimitationConsequence for interpretation
Screening reflects data available at time of checkFindings are point-in-time; re-screening may change a grade
PEP / adverse-media hits are risk indicatorsNot adverse findings in themselves; require verification
Related-party inference is probabilisticA cluster prompts investigation; it is not proof of a relationship
Detection signals are not determinationsEvery signal is reviewable and dispositionable with a recorded rationale
Losses are potential, not confirmed"Cash Out" denotes a paid flagged transaction, not an established loss
