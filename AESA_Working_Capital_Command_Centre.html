1Data foundation

Built on the Acme International schemas: P2P v4.0, O2C v1.2, Inventory v5.03. All tables live under a single client, client = '140' — included in every join and filter.

  • 18 company codes across the group. Always filter by company_code to avoid cross-company mixing.
  • Indian fiscal year (variant V3): April = period 1, March = period 12.
  • Divisions (SPART): Iron Ore (1), Gases (3), Sludge (5), Spares (6), Scrap (8), Pellet (9), Chem (10), Hire (12), Service (13), Wind (15).
  • GL structure from chart BGCA (894 accounts): Assets 1xxxxxx, Liabilities/Equity 2xxxxxx, Revenue 3xxxxxx, Expenses 4xxxxxx.
2Receivables (O2C)

DSO & ageing from the AR subledger — open items bsid, cleared items bsad. Buckets on days past net_due_date.

-- AR ageing by customer (illustrative) SELECT company_code, customer_number, CASE WHEN current_date - net_due_date <= 0 THEN 'not_due' WHEN current_date - net_due_date <= 30 THEN '0_30' WHEN current_date - net_due_date <= 90 THEN '31_90' ELSE '90_plus' END AS bucket, SUM(amount_in_local_currency) AS open_value FROM bsid WHERE client = '140' GROUP BY 1, 2, 3;

DSO = (AR balance ÷ credit revenue) × 365. Days-to-clear = bsad.clearing_date − net_due_date. Terms from knvv.payment_terms_key (Y004=30d, Z007=45d, Z008=60d, Z009=90d). Unearned discount = customers with a discount condition in prcd_elements whose average days-to-clear exceeds their terms. Credit-limit analysis is not possible — knkk & knb5 are empty.

3Payables (P2P)

DPO = (AP balance ÷ COGS) × 365, AP from bseg vendor lines (account_type = 'K'). Early-payment drag compares baseline/due date to actual payment posting.

-- Invoice-over-PO price variance beyond tolerance SELECT e.vendor_id, r.po_doc_number, (r.amount / r.quantity) - (p.net_price / p.price_unit) AS unit_var FROM rseg r JOIN ekpo p ON r.po_doc_number = p.doc_number AND r.po_item_number = p.line_item JOIN ekko e ON p.doc_number = e.doc_number WHERE r.client = '140' AND (r.amount / r.quantity) > (p.net_price / p.price_unit) * 1.02;

Maverick spend = bkpf.transaction_code IN (FB60, FV60, F-53) — no PO, 3-way match bypassed. MSMED guard: DPO extension excludes MSMED-registered vendors, where the statutory 45-day cap applies.

4Inventory (MM)

Movement-based, on the S/4HANA unified table matdoc (replaces the empty legacy MKPF+MSEG). Slow/non-moving = no issue movement within 90 / 180 days; value from mbew.

-- Non-moving stock: no issue movement in 180 days SELECT m.plant, m.material_number, b.standard_price, MAX(m.posting_date) AS last_issue FROM matdoc m JOIN mbew b ON m.material_number = b.material_number AND m.plant = b.valuation_area WHERE m.client = '140' AND m.movement_type IN ('261','601','201') GROUP BY 1, 2, 3 HAVING MAX(m.posting_date) < current_date - 180;

DIO = (inventory value ÷ COGS) × 365. GR/IR gap = MATDOC 101 receipts reconciled against rseg via ekbe. Batch expiry from mcha.shelf_life_expiration. Data reality: MARD unrestricted stock is 0 across all 91,184 rows and 60.8% of materials carry zero standard price — so absence-of-price and MARD balances are not anomaly signals; movement history and valuation are.

5Cash cycle & the cash/EBITDA split

The headline metric is the cash conversion cycle:

CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO = 46 + 67 − 42 = 71 days Cash released = Δcycle days × daily turnover (≈ $7.4M/day)

Why the earlier EBITDA bridge was retired. A previous prototype summed cash-release figures and applied a cost-of-capital to produce a single "EBITDA impact". That conflates two different statements. Changing DPO/DSO/DIO moves cash and the balance sheet — it does not change EBITDA. Only actions that change realised price, discount, or cost touch the P&L. This build keeps them separate: a cash figure ($195M releasable via the cycle) and a distinct, smaller EBITDA figure (~$68M). Neither is double-counted. Cost of capital for any timing-value context is taken at 10% p.a. (illustrative).
