AI Root Cause Summary

Analysis of 191 PROC0324 anomalies identifies six root causes driving early payments. The dominant cause is the absence of a treasury pre-approval control — 188 of 191 transactions (98.4%) were released before the contractual due date with no discount justification and no pre-approval gate to challenge the payment timing.

The second systemic cause is payment term misconfiguration: Z009 terms (46 transactions) show an average of 35 days early — suggesting the net due date calculated by SAP may not reflect the actual contractual agreement, or that AP is not using the baseline date as the payment trigger.

Third, vendor-specific preferential treatment is evidenced by 37 vendors with early payment rates above 50%, and 13 vendors with 3 or more repeat early payments. Ulysses Solutions alone accounts for 10 repeat early payments.

The remaining three causes — period-end acceleration, manual payment overrides, and discount capture failure — are operational process gaps that compound the control weakness.

Root Cause × CoC Exposure Impact

RCA-1 No Treasury Pre-Approval Control

Payments are released to vendors before the contractual due date without any approval gate or challenge mechanism. The AP process executes the payment run based on the document posting date rather than the baseline payment date, resulting in systematic early release.
Evidence: 188 of 191 transactions (98.4%) cleared before due date with no discount justification. No evidence of treasury review or pre-approval in any transaction. All severity levels affected — 9 Critical, 105 High, 75 Medium.
Mapped Remediations
REM-1AImplement SAP payment block (FBZP) on all invoices where clearing date would be >7 days before baseline date. Block requires treasury sign-off to release.Treasury Head4 weeks
REM-1BConfigure automatic payment program (F110) to use baseline payment date as the earliest execution date, not the posting date.SAP Basis / FI Config2 weeks
REM-1CEstablish a weekly pre-payment review report for AP Manager — all invoices scheduled for payment >₹5L within 7 days, flagging those before baseline date.AP Manager1 week

RCA-2 Payment Term Misconfiguration (Z009)

Payment term Z009 shows an average of 35 days early across 46 transactions — significantly higher than the 21.5 day overall average. This suggests the SAP payment term configuration for Z009 does not correctly reflect the actual contractual net due period, or AP operators are overriding the calculated due date.
Evidence: Z009: 46 transactions, avg 35 days early, ₹1.39 Cr invoice value, CoC exposure. Compare Z006: 35 transactions, avg 13 days early. The 22-day gap between Z009 and Z006 is inconsistent with normal term variation.
Mapped Remediations
REM-2AAudit Z009 payment term definition in SPRO (OBB8) — verify net days, baseline date calculation, and discount conditions match the vendor contract.SAP FI Consultant1 week
REM-2BCross-reference Z009 vendor contracts to confirm the net payment period. If contracts say Net-30 but Z009 calculates Net-0, correct the term configuration.Procurement Head3 weeks
REM-2CExtend review to Z050 (5 txns, avg 32 days early, exposure) — same misconfiguration pattern likely applies.SAP FI Consultant1 week

RCA-4 Period-End Payment Acceleration

61% of all early payments (117 of 191) cleared in August 2025, with the remaining 39% concentrated in September. This pattern indicates month-end or period-end AP batch processing that accelerates payments without regard for the contractual due date — likely driven by KPIs that reward invoice processing speed rather than payment timing accuracy.
Evidence: Aug-2025: 117 txns, CoC. Sep-2025: 69 txns, CoC. Oct-Nov: 5 txns combined. Day-of-month distribution: 38% cleared on days 1-10, 35% on days 11-20, 27% on days 21-31 — no clear month-end spike, suggesting the acceleration is period-specific (Aug/Sep) rather than calendar-driven.
Mapped Remediations
REM-4ARevise AP performance KPIs — replace "invoices processed per month" with "payments released on or after baseline date" as the primary AP efficiency metric.CFO / AP Manager4 weeks
REM-4BImplement payment calendar governance — F110 automatic payment runs should not execute for invoices where baseline date is more than 7 days in the future, regardless of period-end pressure.Treasury Head2 weeks

Evidence: CC 3000: 81 txns, CoC, ₹9.47 Cr invoice value. CC 2000: 74 txns, CoC. CC 1000: 36 txns, CoC. CC 3000 uses 9 different payment terms vs CC 1000's 3 — more complex term landscape increases misconfiguration risk.

RCA-6 Near-Zero Discount Capture Rate

Only 3 of 191 transactions (1.6%) captured a cash discount, totalling just in savings. If payments are being made early anyway, the AP process should at minimum be capturing any available discounts to offset the CoC cost — but it is not. This indicates that discount terms are either not configured in SAP or AP is not evaluating discount eligibility before releasing payment.
Evidence: 3 transactions with discount captured: total . 188 transactions with no discount. Payment terms Z008 (70 txns) and Z006 (35 txns) are commonly discount-bearing terms in SAP — yet neither shows meaningful discount capture in the data.
Mapped Remediations
