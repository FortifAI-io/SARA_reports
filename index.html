WELCOME TO STAGE 2 · POST-NDA INTERACTIVE SANDBOX

How to Navigate the Fortifai Agent Experience Cockpit

Experience how a single, zero-egress ERP integration powers specialized AI agents across 4 executive buyers.

Step 01 · Architecture & Suite
Start on the 15-Agent Suite Overview
Review how all 15 agents share one read-only ERP data spine, the Cross-Agent Interlock Map, and the 4-Rung Precedence Waterfall that eliminates double-counting.
Step 02 · Choose Your Buyer Lens
Click CFO, CPO, CCO, or CAE (Left Rail)
Select your executive role in the left navigation bar to unlock the curated AI agents built for your function (e.g., Crystal, Moneta & Kuber for CFO; Aegis & Veritas for CCO).
Step 03 · Hands-On Agent Exploration
Click Sub-Tabs, ⌘K Copilot & Case Rows
Each agent is a 100% live interactive application. Click internal tabs, inspect 3-Arm forensic evidence, test Ask Agent (⌘K) queries, toggle INR / USD, and open Board Reports.
Step 04 · Full-Screen & Fast Switching
Use "⤢ Full Screen" & Top-Bar Pills
The left navigation automatically slims into a compact icon rail when you open an agent. Click ⤢ Full Screen in the top-right to maximize the canvas and switch agents via top-bar pills.
