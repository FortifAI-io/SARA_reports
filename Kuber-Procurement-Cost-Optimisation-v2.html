Methodology, Controls & Data Provenance — Auditor / Regulator Reference

This section documents, at an audit-defensible level, how Fortifai Kuber derives every rupee of flagged opportunity: the deterministic rules behind each of the four commercial levers, the Tier-2 AI pattern-analysis engine, the false-positive and exclusion criteria applied before anything reaches a user, the Impact vs. Ease prioritisation model, and the governance workflow that dispositions each case. Kuber is read-only over a SAP replica; it surfaces signals for human review and posts nothing to the system of record. All figures on this prototype derive from a synthetic dataset and are illustrative.

1 · Three-Stage Processing Architecture

Every signal passes through three stages before a user sees it. This is what separates Kuber from a BI dashboard (which shows raw variance) and from an unconstrained LLM (which hallucinates over SAP tables).

StageFunctionInputs & controls
Stage 1
Ingest		SAP P2P replica + external spine25 SAP tables (EKKO, EKPO, EKBE, RBKP, RSEG, MSEG, BSIK, CDPOS, LFA1) joined on full composite keys (MANDT+BUKRS+GJAHR+BELNR); external commodity indices (LME / ICIS / CMA); D7 supplier-substance (EPFO workforce, GST HSN). Read-only (SELECT).
Stage 2
Condition		Commercial normalisation + noise suppressionTerms-Adjusted Price Bridge (UoM + ZTERM + Incoterms) so prices are compared like-for-like; then the 11 noise suppressants (see §7) strip intercompany, regulated-monopoly, ERS and genuine-emergency traffic.
Stage 3
Triage		Closed-loop human dispositionEach surviving signal is promoted to the Actions Hub (owner + SLA + note, minting a KBR-#### record) or suppressed as a false positive to the Exception Memory Register, with structural-drift tripwires. Full audit lineage retained.
Deterministic-first principle. All monetary math — UoM scaling, credit-carry, freight normalisation, arbitrage, recovery sizing — is computed by deterministic SQL and non-parametric statistics. Generative AI is used only to narrate evidence and draft the renegotiation brief; it never originates a number.
LEVER 1Price Optimisation & PO-to-Invoice Leakage

Detects where paid prices exceed a defensible benchmark after normalising for the three excuses category managers raise — packaging unit, credit terms, and freight. The normalisation bridge is the evidentiary core:

Base UoM Price (P_base) = (EKPO.NETWR_INR) / (EKPO.MENGE × (EKPO.UMREZ / EKPO.UMREN)) Credit Carry Adj (Δ_cred) = P_base × r_WACC(10%) × ((Days(ZTERM_vendor) − Days(ZTERM_benchmark)) / 365) Effective Price (P^eff) = P_base − Δ_cred + Δ_Freight_to_Plant(INCO1)

PRC-02AHard overbillingInvoice unit price exceeds PO unit price beyond SAP tolerance T169G.PROZ2 (2.0%) — a true 3-way-match breachCritical
PRC-02BSub-tolerance micro-creepVariance sits in 0.50%–1.99% (just under the block) across ≥ 80% of a vendor's invoices — invisible to standard 3-way matchHigh

Recovery, not auto-action. For confirmed hard overbilling (PRC-02A), Kuber identifies the recoverable amount and flags the matching open unpaid AP (BSIK) so your team can raise a debit note (FB65) through the normal process ahead of the next payment run. Kuber does not post or net off automatically; recovery is reviewer-confirmed.

CON-03 is a screening signal, not a verdict. EPFO headcount and GST filings are imperfect proxies: an authorised distributor, a consignment/VMI arrangement, or a vendor under a group payroll entity can legitimately show low EPFO. Every CON-03 row is therefore surfaced as an addressable candidate for sourcing review, never as a proven leak.

6 · Tier-2 AI Pattern Analysis

Where Levers 1–4 test explicit rule breaches, the Tier-2 engine detects deviation from each subject's own learned baseline using non-parametric statistics — surfacing schemes that stay within every system tolerance. Tier-2 leads are indicative and are de-duplicated into the 14 Opportunity Packets so the same transaction is never double-counted.

7 · False-Positive & Exclusion Criteria

Before any signal reaches a queue, Kuber evaluates 9 automated noise suppressants carried as line-level flags so legitimate operational traffic is never misclassified.

#SuppressantRationale / SAP basis
1Intercompany & group clearingRelated-party flows (VBUND populated) and internal STOs (ZSTO/UB) are excluded
2Regulated monopolies / state utilitiesPublic utilities & SOEs — price is administered, not negotiable
3ERS / self-billing batchesEvaluated-receipt settlement runs (XERS = 'X') are excluded from invoice-creep flags
4Genuine plant breakdown priorityVerified breakdown maintenance orders carry a legitimate rush requirement
5Regulated professionals & GTA haulageFiduciary retainers (TDS 194J) and primary freight (194C) excluded

9Terms, freight & finding materialityGaps explained by ZTERM credit or INCO1 freight are normalised via the Peff bridge
8 · Governance, Disposition & Continuous Monitoring
  • Promotion to Actions Hub: assigns an owner, wave, and execution note, minting a tracked KBR-#### record with an immutable detected-timestamp.
  • SLA aging vs turnaround (TAT): open items track live age against severity SLAs (Critical 3d · High 7d · Medium 14d). On completion, age stops and closed TAT is permanently locked.
  • Continuous Post-Remediation Monitoring: every identified risk and closed action binds an automated daily regression check (`CM-001+`) so price creep or off-contract bypass cannot silently recur.
