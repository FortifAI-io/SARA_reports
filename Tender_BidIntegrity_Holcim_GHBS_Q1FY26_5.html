Five RFQ clusters were screened during the Q1 FY2026 period. Two clusters (RFQ-001, RFQ-005) showed strong collusion indicators including shared PDF metadata, cover-bid pricing, and related-entity bidders, with one shell entity blocked by D7 verification. The email forensic analysis (raw .eml ingestion) identified three IP-matching bidder groups, adding network-level evidence to the document-level and pricing-level collusion signals. The Calamba Industrial Estate haulage cluster (RFQ-003) is the highest-priority investigation target, with three of five bidders sharing the same /24 subnet, the same industrial estate address, and Villanueva-surnamed UBOs.
